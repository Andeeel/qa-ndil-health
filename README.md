# 🌿 qandil Health (qH)

> A modern, mobile-first health, workout, and wellness application engineered for iOS and Android. Designed to deliver an intuitive, privacy-focused experience for daily fitness management, biometric tracking, and personal growth.

---

## ✨ Core Features & Highlights

### 🔐 User Accounts & Security
* **Authentication:** Secure account management, authentication flows, and session persistence.
* **User Profiles:** Personalized profile settings, customizable goals, and dynamic user preference management.

---

### 🏋️ Workout & Physical Activity Tracking
* **Exercise Logging:** Log exercises across multiple categories including strength training, cardio, flexibility, and high-intensity workouts.
* **Detailed Sets & Reps:** Track targeted metrics including set counts, weight loads, repetition counts, and active exercise durations.
* **Caloric Output:** Real-time calculation and tracking of active calories burned per session.
* **History & Activity Logs:** Scrollable history of past completed workouts with options to update or clear logs.

---

### 📊 Health Analytics & Progress Metrics
* **Dashboard Overview:** Dynamic daily summary showing progress across key health indicators including step counts, hydration, and active minutes.
* **Stats & Trend Visualizations:** Interactive trend charts tracking weekly and monthly activity patterns.
* **Goal Progress Rings:** Visual indicators rendering real-time completion percentages toward daily targets.

---

### 🎨 Custom Design System & Interface
* **Warm Organic Aesthetic:** Custom-crafted color palette featuring signature Bone, Sage, Brass, and Ink design tokens for a clean visual atmosphere.
* **Native Light & Dark Themes:** Automatic and toggleable theme support calibrated for low-light environments and daytime readability.
* **Mobile-First UX:** Form-fitting layout designed specifically for iOS and Android screen dimensions, featuring smooth gesture controls, bottom drawer menus, and safe-area padding.
